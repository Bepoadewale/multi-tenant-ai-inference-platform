## Release-note policy

Every release note records the user-visible change and its evidence boundary under
one of: **Added**, **Changed**, **Deprecated**, **Removed**, **Fixed**, or
**Security**. A release that changes an inference, identity, admission, model-release,
or remediation contract must also state:

- affected endpoint or event contract;
- migration/rollback path;
- local validation performed; and
- whether GPU, cloud, or production evidence remains unexecuted.

Do not report fixture CPU results as GPU capacity, production throughput, or cloud
cost evidence.
